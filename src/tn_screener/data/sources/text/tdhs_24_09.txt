                                  SNAP Citizenship Requirement

Policy 24.09                                                             Eﬀective: August 1, 2020



Introduction
The Tennessee Department of Human Services (TDHS) shall conﬁrm all Supplemental Nutrition
Assistance Program (SNAP) household members meet federal and state guidelines for
citizenship and alien status requirements.
Scope
This policy was developed to outline the requirements for obtaining and verifying eligibility
based on Citizenship and Alien status for all SNAP recipients.
Policy
As a condition of eligibility to receive SNAP, an individual must be a citizen of the United States,
or a qualiﬁed alien who has been lawfully admitted for permanent residence
Verifying Citizenship
An applicant’s statement that all members of the household are U.S. Citizens will be verified only
when the statement is inconsistent or questionable with other information on the application,
on previous applications, or on other documented information known to the worker. When an
applicant’s statement that one or more of the household member’s citizenship is questionable,
the worker will ask the person to provide acceptable verification of citizenship. Acceptable forms
of verification include birth certificates, religious records, voter registration cards, certificates of
citizenship or naturalization provided by United Status of Citizenship and Immigration Services
(USCIS), such as Identification Cards for use of Resident Citizens in the U.S., or U.S. passports.
USCIS documents are the primary source for verifying alien status.
A Qualifying Alien Status of any individual applying for SNAP must be verified prior to approval
or certification. The Systematic Alien Verification for Entitlements (SAVE) system will be used to
validate the alien’s documents and status.
Individuals that are not US Citizens or qualified aliens, or if the worker is unable to verify
immigration status, are ineligible to receive benefits. The income of the excluded household

Deﬁnitions/Acronyms
 Term                        Deﬁnition
 Applicant                   1. An applicant is a person who has submitted a completed and signed document prescribed
                                by TDHS requesting SNAP, and/or a form approved by Food and Nutrition Service (FNS)
                                containing at least a legible name, address and a signature to request SNAP for the
                                household of which he/she is a member.
                             2. An authorized representative or designated agent may actually ﬁle the application provided
                                they have been authorized to do so by the head of the household, spouse, or other
                                responsible household member.

 Application or              An application is a form prescribed by Tennessee Department of Human Services (TDHS) and/or
 Application Form            approved by FNS containing prescribed information which is submitted to the County Oﬃce of
                             the TDHS by a person requesting assistance, or by the person’s legally appointed guardian,
                             designated agent or authorized representative. For Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program
                             (SNAP) purposes, an initial application is the ﬁrst month for which the household applies for
                             participation, following any period during which the household was not certiﬁed for
                             participation in SNAP.

 Household                   A household is a social unit comprised of varying numbers of individuals who live together in
                             the same dwelling. It is also the concept used to determine the food purchasing and
                             preparation patterns of all individuals with common living quarters.
                             For SNAP purposes, a group of people who customarily purchase and prepare food together for
                             home consumption. For program purposes, an individual living alone may be a household.
