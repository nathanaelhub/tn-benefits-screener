                         SNAP GUIDE FOR TENNESSEANS

                     SUPPLEMENTAL NUTRITION ASSISTANCE PROGRAM (SNAP)
         SNAP (Food Stamps) helps people put food on the table. SNAP is the nation's most effective
        anti-hunger program, keeping 8 million American's out of poverty, including 4 million children.
                  Hundreds of thousands of Tennesseans rely on SNAP to escape hunger.
